About Dr. V. Shanta

Dr. V. Shanta is the Chairperson and Senior Consultant in the Department of Paediatric Oncology at Cancer Institute (WIA), Adyar. A legendary figure in Indian oncology, she has devoted over three decades to the fight against cancer and has been instrumental in shaping Cancer Institute (WIA) into one of the most respected cancer centres in the country.

Her pioneering work in paediatric oncology has transformed the care of children with cancer in India. Under her leadership, the institute developed comprehensive treatment protocols for childhood leukaemias, lymphomas, and solid tumours, achieving survival rates comparable to international standards. Dr. Shanta is deeply committed to ensuring that every child, regardless of economic background, receives the best possible cancer treatment.

Recognised with numerous national and international honours for her contributions to cancer care and public health, Dr. Shanta continues to inspire generations of oncologists with her tireless dedication, compassion, and vision for equitable cancer care.

About Dr. S. Krishnamurthy

Dr. S. Krishnamurthy is the Director and Senior Consultant in the Department of Surgical Oncology at Cancer Institute (WIA), Adyar. With over 25 years of dedicated experience in cancer surgery, he has performed thousands of complex oncological procedures and is recognised as one of the leading surgical oncologists in South India.

His clinical expertise spans the full range of solid tumour surgeries, including radical mastectomies, thoracic resections, abdominopelvic surgeries, and minimally invasive oncosurgical procedures. Dr. Krishnamurthy is known for his meticulous surgical technique and his commitment to organ-preserving approaches wherever feasible.

Beyond the operating theatre, he actively mentors young surgeons in training, contributes to multi-disciplinary tumour board discussions, and has published extensively in national and international journals on advances in surgical oncology.

Qualifications

  • MBBS — Madras Medical College, Chennai
  • MS (General Surgery) — University of Madras
  • MCh (Surgical Oncology) — Cancer Institute (WIA), Adyar

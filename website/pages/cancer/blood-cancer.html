+ Blood Cancer

Blood Cancer

Includes leukaemia, lymphoma, and myeloma. Modern therapies including bone marrow transplant offer hope for complete remission.

Understanding Blood Cancer

Blood cancers, also known as haematological malignancies, affect the production and function of blood cells. They originate in the bone marrow, where blood is produced, and disrupt the normal development of healthy blood cells. The three main types are leukaemia (cancer of white blood cells), lymphoma (cancer of the lymphatic system), and myeloma (cancer of plasma cells).

Cancer Institute (WIA) has a long-standing tradition of excellence in treating blood cancers. Our dedicated haematology and bone marrow transplant unit offers state-of-the-art care, from initial diagnosis through complex treatments such as allogeneic and autologous stem cell transplantation. Our laboratory services provide precise diagnosis through flow cytometry, cytogenetics, and molecular testing, enabling personalised therapy selection.

Significant advances in the treatment of blood cancers have transformed outcomes over the past two decades. Targeted therapies like imatinib for chronic myeloid leukaemia, rituximab for lymphomas, and CAR-T cell therapy for refractory cases have dramatically improved survival rates. Our team stays at the forefront of these developments, participating in clinical trials and adopting the latest evidence-based protocols to give our patients the best possible outcomes.

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent fatigue and general weakness
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Frequent or severe infections
  • Easy bruising or unusual bleeding
  • Swollen lymph nodes (neck, armpits, groin)
  • Drenching night sweats and recurrent fevers

Risk Factors

  • Family history of blood cancers
  • Exposure to certain chemicals such as benzene
  • Previous chemotherapy or radiation treatment
  • Exposure to high levels of radiation
  • Certain genetic disorders (Down syndrome, Fanconi anaemia)
  • Smoking and tobacco use

Our Treatment Approach

Multidisciplinary, evidence-based treatment tailored to each patient.

Chemotherapy

Intensive and consolidation chemotherapy protocols tailored to the specific type and stage of blood cancer, including induction regimens for acute leukaemias and combination protocols for lymphomas.

Bone Marrow Transplant

Autologous and allogeneic stem cell transplantation for eligible patients. Our transplant unit provides comprehensive pre-transplant evaluation, conditioning, and post-transplant monitoring.

Targeted Therapy

Molecularly targeted agents such as imatinib for CML and rituximab for B-cell lymphomas, selected based on specific genetic and molecular characteristics of each patient's disease.

Immunotherapy (CAR-T)

Chimeric antigen receptor T-cell therapy for refractory or relapsed blood cancers, harnessing the patient's own immune system to target and destroy cancer cells.

Supportive Care

Blood product transfusions, infection management, growth factor support, nutrition counselling, and psychosocial care throughout the treatment journey.

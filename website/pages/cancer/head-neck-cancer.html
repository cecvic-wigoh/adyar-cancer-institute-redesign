+ Head & Neck Cancer

Head & Neck Cancer

Cancers of the oral cavity, pharynx, larynx, and sinuses. Organ preservation approaches help maintain speech and swallowing function.

Understanding Head & Neck Cancer

Head and neck cancers encompass a group of malignancies that arise in the oral cavity, pharynx (throat), larynx (voice box), paranasal sinuses, and salivary glands. The majority are squamous cell carcinomas that develop in the mucosal lining of these areas. In India, head and neck cancers account for a significant proportion of all cancer diagnoses, largely due to the prevalence of tobacco chewing, smoking, and betel nut use.

At Cancer Institute (WIA), our head and neck oncology programme is one of the most experienced in the country. Our multidisciplinary tumour board brings together surgical oncologists, radiation oncologists, medical oncologists, speech therapists, and reconstructive surgeons to develop comprehensive treatment plans. We place special emphasis on organ preservation strategies that aim to cure the cancer while maintaining essential functions such as speech, swallowing, and breathing.

Modern treatment approaches, including intensity-modulated radiation therapy (IMRT), concurrent chemoradiation, and targeted therapies such as cetuximab, have significantly improved outcomes for patients with head and neck cancers. Our rehabilitation services, including speech therapy and swallowing rehabilitation, ensure that patients receive holistic support throughout their treatment and recovery.

Common Symptoms

  • Non-healing ulcer or sore in the mouth
  • Persistent sore throat lasting more than two weeks
  • Difficulty swallowing or sensation of food getting stuck
  • Voice changes or persistent hoarseness
  • Unexplained ear pain (referred otalgia)
  • A lump or swelling in the neck

Risk Factors

  • Tobacco use (chewing and smoking)
  • Excessive alcohol consumption
  • Human papillomavirus (HPV) infection
  • Betel nut (areca nut) use
  • Poor oral hygiene and dental neglect
  • Occupational exposures (asbestos, wood dust, chemicals)

Our Treatment Approach

Multidisciplinary, evidence-based treatment tailored to each patient.

Surgery (Organ-Preservation)

Minimally invasive and organ-preserving surgical techniques to remove tumours while maintaining speech, swallowing, and breathing functions. Reconstructive surgery available when needed.

Radiation Therapy (IMRT)

Intensity-modulated radiation therapy delivers precise, high-dose radiation to the tumour while sparing surrounding healthy tissues, reducing side effects such as dry mouth and difficulty swallowing.

Chemotherapy

Concurrent chemoradiation protocols using platinum-based agents for locally advanced disease, and induction chemotherapy to shrink large tumours before definitive treatment.

Targeted Therapy

Cetuximab (anti-EGFR therapy) for patients with advanced or recurrent head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, used alone or in combination with radiation or chemotherapy.

Speech Rehabilitation

Comprehensive speech and swallowing rehabilitation by experienced therapists to restore communication and eating abilities after surgery or radiation treatment.

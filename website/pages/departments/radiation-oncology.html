Radiation Oncology

Precise, high-energy radiation therapy that targets tumours while sparing healthy tissue — a cornerstone of curative and palliative cancer care.

About the Department

The Department of Radiation Oncology at Cancer Institute (WIA) has been at the forefront of radiotherapy in India since the 1950s. Today, our department combines decades of clinical expertise with state-of-the-art linear accelerators, treatment planning systems, and quality assurance protocols to deliver highly conformal radiation to tumour targets.

Our radiation oncologists, medical physicists, dosimetrists, and radiation therapists work as a tightly coordinated team. Every treatment plan undergoes rigorous peer review and physics verification before the first fraction is delivered. We offer external beam radiotherapy, brachytherapy (intracavitary and interstitial), and concurrent chemoradiation for a wide range of cancer sites.

The department is also actively involved in clinical research, evaluating hypo-fractionated schedules, adaptive radiotherapy, and novel combinations of radiation with immunotherapy. Our goal is to maximise tumour control while minimising acute and long-term side effects for every patient.

Treatments & Capabilities

IMRT & IGRT

Intensity-modulated and image-guided radiotherapy that shapes radiation beams to the exact contour of the tumour, verified with daily imaging to account for organ motion.

3D Conformal Radiotherapy

CT-based three-dimensional treatment planning that conforms the high-dose radiation volume closely to the tumour shape, reducing exposure to surrounding normal tissues.

Brachytherapy

Internal radiation using sealed radioactive sources placed directly within or adjacent to the tumour — highly effective for cervical, endometrial, and head-and-neck cancers.

Concurrent Chemoradiation

Combined chemotherapy and radiotherapy protocols that enhance tumour sensitivity to radiation, improving cure rates for locally advanced cancers of the head, neck, and cervix.

Conditions We Treat

Breast Cancer

Post-operative & hypo-fractionated whole-breast radiation

Cervical Cancer

External beam RT with intracavitary brachytherapy

Lung Cancer

Stereotactic body radiation & concurrent chemoradiation

Colorectal Cancer

Neoadjuvant pelvic radiation for rectal cancer

Head & Neck Cancer

Definitive & adjuvant IMRT with salivary gland sparing

Prostate Cancer

IMRT with daily IGRT for localised disease

Thyroid Cancer

Adjuvant external beam RT for aggressive histologies

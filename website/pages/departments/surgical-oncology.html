Surgical Oncology

Precision cancer surgery with a commitment to complete tumour removal, organ preservation, and the best possible quality of life.

About the Department

The Department of Surgical Oncology at Cancer Institute (WIA) is one of the oldest and most experienced cancer surgery units in India. Since the Institute's founding in 1954, our surgeons have performed tens of thousands of cancer operations spanning every major organ site, combining technical precision with compassionate patient care.

Our team specialises in curative resections, minimally invasive and laparoscopic procedures, and complex reconstructive surgeries. We work within multidisciplinary tumour boards alongside medical oncologists, radiation oncologists, pathologists, and radiologists to develop individualised treatment plans that give each patient the best chance of cure while preserving function and appearance.

The department is equipped with modern operation theatres, intra-operative monitoring systems, and a dedicated post-operative recovery unit. We are committed to evidence-based practice, regularly auditing our outcomes and participating in national and international surgical oncology research.

Treatments & Capabilities

Curative Tumour Resection

Complete surgical removal of solid tumours with adequate margins, guided by frozen-section pathology to ensure clear boundaries and reduce the risk of recurrence.

Minimally Invasive Surgery

Laparoscopic and video-assisted techniques for selected cancers, offering smaller incisions, less pain, shorter hospital stays, and faster return to daily activities.

Reconstructive & Organ-Preserving Surgery

Breast reconstruction, head-and-neck flap surgery, and sphincter-preserving colorectal procedures that maintain function and quality of life after cancer removal.

Sentinel Lymph Node Biopsy

Targeted lymph node sampling using radiotracer or dye techniques to accurately stage cancer while avoiding unnecessary lymph node removal and its side effects.

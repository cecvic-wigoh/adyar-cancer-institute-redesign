## 

## **. Website Objective**

* Website must be patient-focused and education-oriented.  
* Primary goal: Help patients understand health condition, treatment options, and care journey.  
* Focus on care, trust, and support over promotion.  
* Mandatory regulatory and disclosure requirements must be followed.  
* Full patient journey to be reflected: Prevention → Diagnosis → Treatment → Recovery → Support.

2\. **Website Structure (Main Navigation – Proposed)**

* About Us \-  
* Departments  
* Research  
*  For patients  
*  For Doctors  
* Academics—\>,, For students   
* Resources  
* Patient Education  
* News & Events  
* Careers  
* Contact us/ What you can do? 

## **. Homepage Requirements**

* Homepage to be patient-centric and easy to navigate.  
* Must clearly answer:  
  * What problem does the patient have?  
  * How can we help?  
* Dynamic sections:  
  * Patient Education  
  * Events  
  * Testimonials  
  * Success Stories  
* Sections to support updates every 1–2 weeks.  
* Support local language testimonials.  
* Include:  
  * Search / Chatbot  
  * FAQ section  
* FAQs to cover:  
  * CT/MRI safety  
  * Biopsy process  
  * Waiting time  
  * Insurance  
  * Parking  
  * Government schemes

## **6\. Patient Education & Content Guidelines**

* Patient education is a core website feature.  
* Content must be simple, non-technical, and easy to understand.  
* Pages to explain:  
  * Cancer types  
  * Symptoms  
  * Treatment options  
  * Side effects  
* Clear care pathways for common conditions.  
* Focus on treatment process and support systems over individual profiles.

## **7\. Department Responsibilities**

* Each department to provide:  
  * Required content  
  * Design inputs  
  * Page-level suggestions  
* One representative per department to coordinate.  
* Inputs to be submitted within 1–2 weeks.  
* All inputs to be updated only in shared Docs/Sheets.

## **8\. Data, Reports & Compliance**

* Mandatory data to be displayed:  
  * Number of surgeries  
  * Patient statistics  
  * Clinical details  
* Annual reports (from 2021 onwards) to be uploaded.  
* Cancer-related statistics to be highlighted.  
* System must support easy future updates.

## **9\. Feedback & Patient Engagement**

* Website to include feedback form.  
* QR code integration for offline feedback.  
* Feedback data to be accessible for internal review.  
* Suggestions to be used for continuous improvement.

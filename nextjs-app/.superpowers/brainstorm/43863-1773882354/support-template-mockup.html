About the Department

Department Overview

Cancer Institute (WIA) believes that Clinical Excellence and Delivery of High Quality of Care are fundamental criteria of a successful destination of choice for Cancer treatment in the country.

Implementation of clinical practice guidelines based on the best available scientific evidence, together with measuring quality monitors and acting on the data, is crucial for continuous quality improvement and patient safety.

Our Purpose

Mission & Philosophy

For us, quality is both a responsibility we lead and a promise we make to every patient and family who entrust us with their care. We strive to embed a culture of quality and safety that touches every corner of the institution.

Patient Safety

Ensuring the highest standards of safety through systematic monitoring, risk assessment, and prevention protocols.

Continuous Improvement

Measuring quality monitors and acting on data to drive evidence-based improvements across all departments.

Patient-Centered Care

Putting patients and families at the centre of every quality initiative and care pathway design.

Our Framework

Six Dimensions of Quality Healthcare

Safe

Avoiding harm to patients from the care intended to help them

Effective

Providing services based on scientific knowledge and best practices

Efficient

Avoiding waste of resources including equipment, supplies, and time

Equitable

Providing care that does not vary in quality because of personal characteristics

Timely

Reducing waits and harmful delays for both patients and caregivers

Patient-Centered

Providing care that is respectful of individual patient preferences and values

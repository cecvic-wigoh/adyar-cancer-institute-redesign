About the Department

Department Overview

The Department of Radiology specializes in medical imaging that helps detect, diagnose, and monitor cancer. Using technologies such as X-rays, mammography, ultrasound, CT and MRI, we provide detailed images and expert reports that guide doctors in making accurate treatment decisions.

Radiology plays a crucial role in every stage of cancer care — from early detection and diagnosis to treatment planning, monitoring treatment response, and long-term follow-up.

What to Expect During Your Visit

Understanding your imaging procedure — a simple guide for patients and families

1

Before Your Scan

Your referring doctor will explain which imaging test is needed. Some scans require fasting or specific preparation — our team will provide clear instructions when you schedule.

2

During the Procedure

Most imaging tests are painless and non-invasive. You may need to lie still for a few minutes while the scanner captures images. Our technologists ensure your comfort throughout.

3

After Your Scan

Our radiologists analyze your images and prepare a detailed report for your treating doctor. Results are typically available within 24-48 hours.

Diagnostic Services & Techniques

  • MRI — Brain, spine, breast, abdomen, pelvis, extremities

Conventional & Screening
  • Digital Mammography & Tomosynthesis
  • Digital X-ray systems
  • Ultrasound — 2D and 3D with Doppler
  • Contrast-enhanced Ultrasound (CEUS)

  • Image-guided biopsies (USG, CT, stereotactic)
  • Tumor embolization
  • Microwave ablation
  • Drainage procedures & stent placements
  • Fiducial marker placement
  • Vascular access procedures (PICC, ports)
Treatment Capabilities

Therapeutic Services

Image-Guided Biopsies

Precise tissue sampling using ultrasound, CT, or stereotactic guidance for accurate cancer diagnosis.

Tumor Embolization

Cutting off blood supply to tumors to shrink them before surgery or as a standalone treatment.

Microwave Ablation

Minimally invasive tumor destruction using heat, guided by real-time imaging.

Vascular Access

PICC lines, port placements, and other vascular access procedures for chemotherapy delivery.
